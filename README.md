# 🏫 Emoji High School

**One school year. Six classmates. The only language you speak is emoji.**

Everyone is hiding what they love.
Every emoji has a club, a colour, a size and some tags, and none of that is hidden.
So send someone a picture and read the face that comes back:
🍓 landing well could mean *red*, *food*, or *tiny*. Send another to find out which.

Nobody's taste is written down anywhere.
It's rolled from a random seed, so no guide can spoil it.
You have forty weeks to figure these people out.
On the final day you can hand one of them the emoji they love most in the world and win their heart.

Your school year saves itself at the end of every week!

Recommended running in Chrome browser. Works on phones too.

Created by Frank Force for JS13k 2026

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Mouse or Touch** = Everything. The whole game is one phone.
- **🏠** = Back to the home screen
- **★** = An app with a slot you haven't used yet
- **🔋** = The week still has something in it
- **🔊 🎵** = Sound and music
- **🌈 load Twemoji font** = the first thing on the home screen. Tap it once and the
  whole game switches to Twemoji, and it remembers. Everything in a js13k entry has
  to fit in the 13kb, and a font does not, so the game never fetches it unless you
  ask - until you do, the emoji are whichever ones your own device draws.

## 📱 How to Play

Every week has three slots, and nothing carries over.

- **🏫 School.** Pick a club, learn one of the three emoji it offers. Your collection is everything you're able to say.
- **💬 The phone.** *One* action. Text someone, give a present, or ask someone out. Answering a text somebody sent you costs that same action.
- **🌤️ The weekend.** The date you booked, or a trip alone for emoji the school won't teach you.

A message is two slots, and each one holds an emoji or a face:

- 🙂 **just saying**: halves the score, hit *or* miss
- 😍 **I mean it**: doubles it, both ways
- ❓ **why?**: they name the opinion your emoji touched, in words

Play it safe while you're guessing. Once you're sure, say it like you mean it.
Watch what they open with, keep an eye on their mood, and read 📓 Notes, which remembers every reaction you ever got.

## 🌈 Features

- Six classmates whose taste is rolled fresh every run
- 152 emoji sharing 45 attributes, so what you learn about one tells you about others
- Forty weeks of clubs, exams, dates, trips, birthdays, presents and jealousy
- Five moods, and classmates who give up on you if you go quiet
- An emoji dictionary that spells out what everything is
- Unicorn Week 🦄, a free emoji in week thirteen that goes with everything
- Three endings, a yearbook, and a letter under a tree
- Endless procedurally generated music
- ZzFX sound effects
- Twemoji art on request - one tap, and the cast looks the same on every machine
- All written from scratch in vanilla JS
